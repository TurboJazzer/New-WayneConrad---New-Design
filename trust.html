Trust and Data

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Wayne Conrad Advisory is a solo, remote-first practice. I would rather show you how I work than ask you to take it on trust. This page collects the commitments I make about your data, the AI tools I use and what happens if something does not work. The binding terms are in the terms of service and the written agreement for each project.

Your data stays in your accounts

  • Everything is built in your own accounts, through credentials you grant and can revoke at any time.
  • Ownership of what I deliver passes to you on final payment.
  • I send AI tools only the fields a workflow needs.

Before any data is shared

  • A data processing agreement is signed before I see any client data.
  • The written agreement names every tool that may handle your data. Ask for my sub-processor list at any time.
  • I use business or API accounts only, which do not train on your content by default.
  • I keep your information confidential.

When the project ends

When the support window ends I remove my access and delete project copies, or earlier if you ask. Backups and records I am legally required to keep are the exception.

A person stays in control

AI output can be wrong, so tests and approval steps are built in. You set the limits. The automation handles what falls inside them and routes only the larger decisions to you for sign-off. Approvals and judgement stay with people.

What the guarantee covers

I guarantee the build, not your results. Written acceptance criteria are agreed before work starts. If the delivered work does not meet them, I fix it at no charge for up to 10 working days from your written notice, given within 14 days of handover. If it still does not meet them, I refund the fee you paid for that sprint. The guarantee does not cover problems caused by your changes, late access or approvals, incorrect data, or changes by third-party software or services. I do not promise revenue, savings or hours saved. My total liability for a project is limited to the fees you paid for it.

What I do not claim

  • I do not hold a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate. The vendors I use publish their own.
  • I am a sole practitioner, not a large firm.

Data collected on this website

Your diagnostic answers and email address are processed through Anthropic's API to write your report. I use API access, not a consumer chat product. Analytics cookies are not set until you accept them. The privacy policy lists every service provider and your rights.
