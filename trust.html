Trust and data

How your data is handled and who stays in control

By Wayne Conrad  ·  October 17, 2026  ·  3 min read

Short answer: an agreement is signed before I see any client data, every tool that may handle it is named in writing, AI workflows keep a person in the approval loop, and you own what is built. I hold no security certification, and this page says so plainly.

How your data is handled

  • I sign a data processing agreement before I see any client data.
  • I name every tool that may handle your data in the written agreement before any data is shared.
  • I use business or API accounts, which do not train on your content by default, and send tools only the fields a workflow needs.
  • Access is through credentials you grant and can revoke, and it is scoped to the workflow.
  • When the support window ends I remove my access and delete project copies, or earlier if you ask. Backups and records I am legally required to keep are the exception.
  • The sub-processors I use for this website are listed below.

Sub-processors for this website

  • Netlify: website hosting and the functions that run the diagnostic and resource requests.
  • Anthropic: writes the diagnostic report from your answers, through its API under commercial terms.
  • Resend: sends the report and resource emails.
  • HubSpot: call booking.
  • Google Analytics and Tag Manager: usage analytics, only after you accept cookies.

Data processing agreement

The agreement I sign before I see any client data is published in full: read the data processing agreement or download the PDF. It covers instructions, confidentiality, security, sub-processors, breach notice within 48 hours, deletion, audits, and international transfers (the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, POPIA and the Australian Privacy Principles), plus the US state-law service-provider terms.

You contract with Wayne Conrad Advisory, a division of Poseidon Network Systems CC, South Africa.

Where a person stays in control

  • Approval steps sit where your judgement matters. The workflow drafts, checks the limits and routes the work, and a person approves the steps that cannot be undone.
  • Decision limits are written down first. Only what is above them is routed to a person.
  • AI tools can produce wrong output. That is why approval steps keep a person in control, and why I do not promise revenue, savings or hours saved.
  • The free AI hand-off checklist shows the five questions to answer before any process is handed to AI.

Who owns what

  • Everything is built in your own accounts. Ownership transfers at final payment.
  • You pay the tool vendors directly. Typical monthly running costs are estimated on the pricing page.
  • A sprint ends at handover with a recorded walkthrough and a one-page runbook. Anything after that is a separate written agreement.

What happens to your diagnostic answers

  • They are used only to write your report, which is shown on the page and emailed to you.
  • They are not shared with anyone else, apart from the two services that process them for me: Anthropic writes the report text and Resend sends the email. I delete them if you ask.
  • A software service writes the report text from your answers.

What I do not claim

  • I do not hold a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate. The vendors I use publish their own.
  • I guarantee the build, not your results. The guarantee, its limits and the refund terms are on the pricing page.

Read the privacy policy and the terms of service for the full legal wording.
