The price is confirmed in writing before work starts. Invoices are due on the dates in the written agreement. Prices exclude taxes and the cost of any software, licences or usage, which you pay directly to the vendors.

The work is built in your accounts. Ownership of what I deliver passes to you on final payment. I keep ownership of my own pre-existing methods and tools, and third-party software stays under its own licence.

5. The build guarantee

I guarantee the build, not your results. If the delivered work does not meet the agreed acceptance criteria, I fix it at no charge for up to 10 working days from your written notice.
