Panel 03 · Not included

What is not included

  • Ongoing retainers. A sprint ends at handover. Anything after that is a separate written agreement.
  • Software licences and usage charges. You pay the tool vendors directly, in your own accounts.
Panel 04 · What you provide

What you provide

  • A signed data processing agreement before any data is shared.
  • The tools each workflow runs in, named in writing, in your own business accounts.
  • Credentials you grant and can revoke at any time.
  • Timely access and approvals, so the dates hold.
  • Accurate source data, and confirmation that you have the right to share it.
Panel 05 · The guarantee

I guarantee the build, not your results.

  • Written acceptance criteria are agreed before work starts. They change only by written agreement.
  • If the delivered work does not meet them, I fix it at no charge for up to 10 working days from your written notice.
  • If it still does not meet them, I refund the fee you paid for that sprint. That fix or refund is your only remedy for the work missing its criteria.
  • This does not cover problems caused by your changes, late access or approvals, incorrect data, or changes by third-party software or services.
  • Tell me in writing, naming the criterion, within 14 days of handover. During the support window I also fix defects, meaning anything that does not meet an agreed criterion.
  • I do not promise revenue, savings or hours saved. AI output can be wrong, so tests and approval steps are built in.
Panel 06 · Data handling

How your data is handled

  • An agreement is signed before any data is shared.
  • I name every tool that may handle your data in the written agreement before any data is shared. Business or API tiers only.
  • I use the minimum data a workflow needs.
  • Access is scoped and you can revoke it at any time.
  • Project copies are deleted when the support window ends, or earlier if you ask.

I hold no security certification. The vendors I use publish their own.

Panel 07 · Questions

Questions about price and terms

How much does each sprint cost?

The three sprints start from $1,500, $2,900 and $8,500. The price depends on the number of systems, integrations and monthly volume. I confirm it in writing before work starts.

Are there ongoing fees?

No retainer is included. If you want me to keep going after a sprint, that is a separate written agreement. Software licences and usage charges are paid directly to the tool vendors. Typical tool and usage cost after handover is about $40 to $150 a month for a single AI workflow and $150 to $500 for the end-to-end AI ops engine.

What does the guarantee cover?

I guarantee the build, not your results. Written acceptance criteria are agreed before work starts and change only by written agreement. If the work misses them I fix it at no charge for up to 10 working days from your written notice, and if it still misses them I refund the fee you paid for that sprint. That is your only remedy for the work missing its criteria. It does not cover problems caused by your changes, late access or approvals, incorrect data, or changes by third-party software or services.

What happens after the sprint?

You get a 30 or 90 day window, depending on the sprint, where I fix anything that does not meet the agreed criteria. After that you keep it as it is or ask me to continue.

Who owns what you build?

You do. It is built in your accounts and ownership transfers at final payment.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I handle the build and the handover. The automation asks for sign-off only above limits you set, and sends a short report.

Is my data safe?

I sign a data processing agreement before I see any client data. I use business or API accounts and send tools only the fields a workflow needs. I name every tool that may handle your data in the written agreement before any data is shared. Access is through credentials you grant and can revoke. When the support window ends I remove my access and delete project copies, or earlier if you ask. Backups and records I am legally required to keep are the exception. I do not hold a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate; the vendors I use publish their own.

Can I start with something smaller?

Yes. Book a free 15-minute call or take the 2-minute diagnostic and I will tell you which sprint, if any, fits.
