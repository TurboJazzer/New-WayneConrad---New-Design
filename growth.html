Where AI Takes Over the Manual Work

As a service business grows to 15, 30 or 50 people, the coordination grows with it: chasing replies, onboarding steps, status updates, invoices and approvals. AI implementation done well fixes the process first, then automates it, so the workflow keeps running whoever is in the room.
