Getting started

What this is, and whether it applies to your business.

What does it mean if my business has founder dependency?

It means decisions, approvals, and daily operations still route through you by default, even in areas where someone else could handle them. The test is simple: could the business run at full quality for two weeks if you were unreachable. Most owners already know the answer before they take the diagnostic.

What is the free AI readiness assessment?

Five questions that map exactly where your business depends on your personal availability rather than your judgment, and what that dependency is costing you in time and margin. It produces a plain-language report, not a generic score, and takes about three minutes.

Is Wayne Conrad Advisory available if my business is not based in the USA, UK, or Australia?

Yes. The practice works with SME owners in the USA, UK, Australia, and Southeast Asia, and is run remote-first across time zones. Location has never limited the engagement.

No. The process starts with mapping the decision and the process, not the software. AI gets implemented afterward, only where it actually fits. You are not expected to understand the mechanics, only to know where the bottlenecks sit in your own business.

What happens after I take the diagnostic?

You get a personalised report naming the specific gap and what to fix first. There is no sales call required to receive it, and no obligation to book anything afterward.

How long does an engagement typically run?

It depends on how many processes need redesigning and how deep the dependency runs. That gets scoped on the call itself, once the diagnostic has named what actually needs fixing. Engagements are built around specific bottlenecks, not open-ended retainers, and you own what gets built at the end.
