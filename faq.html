Getting started

What this is, and whether it applies to your business.

What can AI automate in a service business?

AI can take over repeatable work that follows rules: replying to and scoring enquiries, onboarding a new client, pulling together weekly reports, and raising invoices and chasing payment. Judgement calls such as pricing exceptions stay with people, and the automation routes only the ones above limits you set to you for approval. The diagnostic shows which workflow is worth automating first in your business.

What is the free diagnostic?

Five questions that show which of your workflows still depend on manual effort, and what that is costing you in time and margin. It produces a plain-language report, not a generic score, and takes about two minutes.

Is Wayne Conrad Advisory available if my business is not based in the USA, UK, or Australia?

Yes. The practice works with SME owners in the USA, UK, Australia, and Southeast Asia, and is run remote-first across time zones. Location has never limited the engagement.

How it works

The process, and what's expected of you.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to work with an AI implementation advisor?

No. AI automation is the build. I map the process first so it runs reliably, then build it in your own accounts. You are not expected to understand the mechanics, only to know where the manual work piles up in your business.

What happens after I take the diagnostic?

You get a personalised report naming the first workflow to automate and what the manual work is costing you. There is no sales call required to receive it, and no obligation to book anything afterward.

How long does an engagement typically run?

It depends on how many workflows are in scope and how many systems they touch. That gets scoped on the call itself, once the diagnostic has named what actually needs fixing. Engagements are built around specific bottlenecks, not open-ended retainers, and you own what gets built at the end.
