Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what data wayneconrad.com collects, why, and the choices you have. Wayne Conrad Advisory is a solo, remote-first consultancy serving SME owners in the USA, UK, Australia, and Southeast Asia.

What I collect

  • Information you submit directly, such as your name and email address when you complete the Founder Bottleneck Scorecard, subscribe to updates, or book a call.
  • Usage data collected automatically through Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, such as pages visited, time on site, and general location (country/city level, derived from IP address).

Cookies

This site uses cookies from Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the pages. These cookies are not set until you accept them via the cookie banner shown on your first visit.

Service Purpose Typical duration
Google Analytics (GA4) Understand visitor behavior and site performance Up to 2 years
Google Tag Manager Manages the tags above; does not itself set cookies N/A

You can withdraw or change your cookie choice at any time using the button below, or via "Manage Cookies" in the footer of any page.

How I use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and deliver requested resources (such as the diagnostic results and nurture emails).
  • To understand which content and pages are useful, so the site can be improved.
  • I do not sell personal data, and I do not share it with third parties beyond the service providers listed below.

Service providers

I use the following third-party services to operate this site and its funnel. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Netlify — website hosting
  • Google Analytics / Tag Manager — site usage analytics (only after cookie consent)
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Kit (ConvertKit) — email subscriber management
  • Airtable — subscriber records
  • Calendly — call scheduling

Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent for analytics tracking at any time. To exercise these rights, contact me using the details below.

Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, reach out via LinkedIn or through the contact details on the Advisory Services page.
