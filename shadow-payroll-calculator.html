Calculate what manual coordination is actually costing your business. Enter your team roles, hourly cost, and hours spent on administrative work each week. The number usually surprises people, and it's the number that should come before any AI implementation, not after.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter each person or role in your team in Section 1. Include their fully loaded cost per hour (salary plus on-costs divided by annual hours).
  2. In Section 2, estimate how many hours per week each role spends on work that could be handled by a repeatable process. This includes manual reporting, data transfer, chasing approvals, formatting documents, and administrative coordination.
  3. The results panel calculates your weekly and annual manual coordination cost automatically.
  4. Print to PDF using your browser (File → Print → Save as PDF) and share with your leadership team.
