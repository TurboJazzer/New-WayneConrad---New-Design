Panel 01 · The pattern

Four workflows still run by hand

  1. 1
    Payments: invoices go out late and reminders depend on someone remembering.

    The manual workflow: raise, send, chase.

    What the AI does

    Sends invoices when work completes, schedules reminders and drafts the overdue messages.

  2. 2
    Reporting: someone checks back-office work every evening.

    The manual workflow: check every item, then summarise.

    What the AI does

    Checks each item and reports only the exceptions.

  3. 3
    Enquiries: discounts and exceptions wait for a reply.

    The manual workflow: each one stalls until someone says yes.

    What the AI does

    Routes each one against written limits and asks for sign-off only above them.

  4. 4
    Continuity: nobody can say what happens when work stops for two weeks.

    The manual workflow: it lives in people's heads.

    What the AI does

    The workflows keep running, and anything needing sign-off reaches your phone.

Panel 07 · Questions

Questions owners ask before they book

How much does an AI workflow sprint cost?

The three sprints start from $1,500, $2,900 and $8,500. The price depends on the number of systems and how much runs through them. I confirm it in writing before work starts.

Isn't AI expensive?

Sprints are fixed price from $1,500, confirmed in writing before work starts. Software licences and usage charges are paid directly to the vendors, and every tool is named in writing before we start. You own what is built and there is no lock-in. Typical running costs are on the pricing page.

Which tools do you work with?

HubSpot, Asana, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Slack, Make, n8n, Xero and Salesforce. If you use something else, ask on the free call.

Who owns what you build?

You do. It is built in your accounts and ownership transfers at final payment.

What happens after the sprint?

You have a tuning window of 30 days (90 days for the end-to-end AI ops engine) where I fix anything that does not meet the agreed criteria. After that you keep it as it is or ask me to continue.

Do I need to be technical?

No. I map the workflow, build it in your accounts and hand over a recorded walkthrough and a one-page runbook. You read a short report and approve anything above your limits.

Will this replace my staff?

It removes copy-and-paste coordination. Approvals and judgement stay with people.

Is my data safe?

I sign a data processing agreement before I see any client data. I use business or API accounts, which do not train on your content by default, and send tools only the fields a workflow needs. I name every tool that may handle your data in the written agreement before any data is shared. Access is through credentials you grant and can revoke. When the support window ends I remove my access and delete project copies, or earlier if you ask. Backups and records I am legally required to keep are the exception. I do not hold a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate; the vendors I use publish their own. Ask for my sub-processor list at any time.

What happens after the diagnostic?

You get a report straight away. No sales call is needed.

Do you work outside the USA, UK and Australia?

Yes, including Southeast Asia. I work remotely.
