AI Workflow Candidate Map

List the workflows that take the most manual hours, then score each one for AI automation: what starts it, which tools it runs in, how much time it takes and where a person should sign off.

How to use this map

  1. Fill in your business details at the top.
  2. Work through the five common workflows. For each one, record what starts it, the tools it runs in, how often it happens and how many hours it takes by hand each week.
  3. Score the risk if the output is wrong and decide where a person should sign off. Those sign-off limits become the approval steps built into the automation.
  4. Complete the summary. It totals your manual hours and names the workflow to automate first.
  5. Print to PDF using your browser (File, Print, Save as PDF) and bring it to your free 15-minute workflow call.
