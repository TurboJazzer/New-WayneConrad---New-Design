Client Health Scorecard

Surface the early warning signals of client disengagement before a relationship is lost. Score each client weekly across six signals that predict churn before it happens.

Wayne Conrad Advisory Fill in on screen then print to PDF Review weekly with your team

How to use this scorecard

  1. Add each active client relationship you want to track.
  2. For each client, rate the six signals based on what you have actually observed in the last two to four weeks, not what you assume or hope is true.
  3. The results panel calculates a health status for each client automatically.
  4. Clients flagged as "At Risk" or "Watch" are worth a direct conversation before the relationship cools further.
  5. Print to PDF and review with your team weekly or fortnightly. Five minutes per client is enough.
