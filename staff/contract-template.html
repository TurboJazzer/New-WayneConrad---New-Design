3. Acceptance criteria (Schedule A)

The agreed criteria are listed in Schedule A. Each is numbered, measurable, one line, with the way it is tested. They change only by written agreement.

4. Fees and payment schedule

Fee: [$ amount], in USD. The fee excludes taxes and software costs you pay direct to vendors.

Payment: 50% on signing and 50% on handover, unless this schedule says otherwise: [any different split].

Work starts when the first payment clears.

5. Milestones and dates (Schedule B)

The milestones and their dates are in Schedule B.

6. Client responsibilities

You give timely access to the systems and people I need, approve drafts within [3] working days, and supply accurate data. Delays on your side move the dates and fall outside the guarantee.

7. Guarantee

If delivered work misses an agreed criterion, I fix it free for up to 10 working days from your written notice. If it still misses, I refund the fee you paid for that sprint. That fix or refund is the only remedy.

You must give written notice naming the criterion within 14 days of handover.

Not covered: your changes, late access or approvals, incorrect data, and third-party changes. I make no promise of revenue, savings or hours.

8. Support window

Support window: [30 / 30 / 90] days from handover. I fix defects free during this time.

9. Data handling

The tools that may handle your data are named in Schedule C. I delete my copies when the support window ends, or earlier on your request. Backups and records the law requires me to keep are excepted.

10. Ownership

I build the work in your accounts. Ownership of the deliverables passes to you on final payment. I keep my methods and tools. Third-party software stays under its own licence.

11. Cancellation and termination

Either of us may cancel at any time by written notice. You pay for work done up to the notice date and for third-party costs already committed. The first payment is not refundable once work has started, unless I cancel without cause or the guarantee applies. Either of us may end this agreement for a material breach that is not fixed within 10 working days of written notice.

12. Late payment and suspension

If an undisputed invoice is more than 14 days overdue, I may pause work and hold handover until it is paid. The guarantee clock stops while payment is overdue. Interest runs at 1.5% a month, or the legal maximum if lower. [Lawyer to confirm]

13. Liability

My total liability is limited to the fees you paid for the project. I am not liable for indirect or consequential loss, to the extent the law allows. Nothing in this agreement limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

14. General terms

Confidentiality. Each of us keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only for this project. [Lawyer to confirm wording]

AI output. A person checks AI output before it goes live. Results vary.

Governing law. [To be set by lawyer]

Entire agreement. This agreement and its schedules are the whole agreement and replace earlier discussions.

Changes. Changes must be in writing and agreed by both of us.
