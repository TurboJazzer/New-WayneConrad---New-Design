4.Fees and payment schedule

6.Client responsibilities

You give timely access, approve drafts within [3] working days and supply accurate data. Delays move the dates and sit outside the guarantee.

7.Guarantee

If the delivered work misses an agreed criterion, I fix it free for up to 10 working days from your written notice. If it still misses, I refund the fee you paid for that sprint. That fix or refund is the only remedy. Give written notice naming the criterion within 14 days of handover.

Not covered: your changes, late access or approvals, incorrect data, and changes by third parties. I do not promise revenue, savings or hours saved.

8.Support window

[30 / 30 / 90] days. Defects are fixed free.

9.Data handling

Tools that may handle your data are named in Schedule C. Project copies are deleted when the support window ends, or earlier on request. Backups and records the law requires me to keep are the exception.

10.Ownership

The work is built in your accounts. Ownership of the deliverables passes to you on final payment. I keep my methods and tools. Third-party software stays under its own licence.

11.Cancellation and termination

Either of us can cancel at any time by written notice. You pay for work done up to the notice date and for third-party costs already committed. The first payment is not refundable once work has started, unless I cancel without cause or the guarantee applies. Either party may end this agreement for a material breach that is not fixed within 10 working days of written notice.

12.Late payment and suspension

If an undisputed invoice is more than 14 days overdue I may pause work and hold handover until it is paid. The guarantee clock stops while payment is overdue. Interest is 1.5% a month, or the legal maximum if lower. [Lawyer to confirm].

13.Liability

My total liability is limited to the fees you paid for the project. I am not liable for indirect or consequential loss, to the extent the law allows. Nothing here limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

14.Confidentiality, AI output and general terms

Each of us keeps the other's confidential information private. A person checks AI output before it goes live, and results vary. Governing law: [to be set by lawyer]. This is the entire agreement. Changes must be in writing.
